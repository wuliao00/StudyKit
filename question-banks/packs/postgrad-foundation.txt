[考研·英语一·主观] Write an essay of about 160 words on how to evaluate information online.|Online information is plentiful but uneven, so the first question is not whether a claim sounds right but where it comes from. I check the source: a named author, a date, and references count for more than confident wording. Second, I look for the same fact in an independent outlet, because errors spread when everyone copies one report. Third, I separate description from persuasion; emotional language is a signal to slow down. Fourth, I ask what evidence would change my mind. If nothing could, I am not evaluating the claim, only defending a position I already hold. A useful test is to imagine the same sentence applied to a subject I care about: would I still accept the evidence, or would I ask for more? That question exposes most weak claims, and it takes only a moment. These habits cost a few minutes, but they are far cheaper than acting on a rumor and correcting it later.|观点明确；有可操作的判断标准；论证有层次；语言准确；词数接近要求。
